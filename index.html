Raye's Story

RAYE, also known as Rachel Keen, is a singer-songwriter from South London who strongly refused to be silenced. She regained control after years of writing for the most prominent singers in the world while her own voice went unnoticed.

Her debut album My 21st Century Blues would pave the way for her current success. Now, Raye continues to chart as a number one artist in the UK.
